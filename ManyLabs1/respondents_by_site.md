# ManyLabs1 respondents by site

Respondents per site in the three ManyLabs1 studies that Experiments I and III of the paper use. The target site of both experiments is `pi`, and the other 35 sites are sources.

The counts are the rows of `pre-process/Manylabs1_data.pkl`, before OSRCT selection draws the observational samples. The `data` stage of the [reproduction scripts](../paper/README.md) builds that file from the raw ManyLabs1 data ([download instructions](../osrct_benchmark_v1.0/data/README.md)).
